About Me

Dedicated to advancing visual understanding through principled empirical research

Research Vision

I am driven by the challenge of making deep learning models truly understand visual information the way humans do. My work bridges the gap between theoretical advances in neural architectures and their practical deployment in resource-constrained, real-world environments. I believe that understanding why models fail is as important as improving their accuracy.

Current Focus

Currently, I am deeply engaged in studying adversarial robustness in deep neural networks — understanding how small, imperceptible perturbations can fool state-of-the-art vision models, and developing defense mechanisms. I am also exploring cross-domain transfer learning to adapt pretrained visual representations to new domains with minimal labeled data.

Research Areas

Five interconnected research themes guiding my doctoral journey

01
Architecture Analysis
Deep Learning Architectures for Vision

Analyzing, comparing, and improving CNNs and Vision Transformers for image understanding. Investigating design choices, training strategies, and efficiency trade-offs between convolutional and attention-based approaches.

CNNs Vision Transformers ResNet ViT
02
Transfer Learning
Cross-Domain Transfer Learning

Adapting pretrained visual representations to new domains with minimal labeled data. Focus on medical imaging, satellite imagery, and industrial inspection where labeled data is scarce but domain shift is extreme.

Fine-tuning Few-shot Learning Domain Adaptation
03
Interpretability
Model Behavior & Interpretability

Understanding what vision models actually learn, identifying their failure modes, and characterizing internal representations through feature visualization, attention maps, and linear probing classifiers.

Feature Visualization Attention Maps Failure Analysis
04
Robustness
Robustness Under Real-World Degradation

Evaluating and improving model performance under noise, blur, compression artifacts, and distribution shifts. Currently studying FGSM and PGD adversarial attacks to understand model vulnerabilities and develop certified defenses.

Adversarial Attacks FGSM PGD Image Corruptions
05
Deployment
Efficient & Deployable Vision Systems

Optimizing models for resource-constrained environments without accuracy loss. Exploring model compression, pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation for edge devices and embedded systems in healthcare and autonomous systems.

Model Compression Quantization Edge Deployment

Doctoral Research Proposal

I have prepared a comprehensive doctoral research proposal focusing on advancing visual understanding through principled empirical research. The proposal outlines a 3-year timeline with five phases: Foundation & Baseline, Architecture Analysis, Cross-Domain Transfer, Robustness Analysis, and Efficiency & Deployment. Key proposed contributions include a hybrid CNN-ViT architecture and a multi-task self-supervised pre-training framework for both convolutional and attention-based features.

Hybrid CNN-ViT Self-Supervised Learning 3-Year Timeline Target: CVPR/ICCV/ECCV
